Welcome to my blog! I'm excited to share my thoughts and experiences on programming languages, dataflow systems, and research in computer science.

## What to Expect

In this blog, I'll be writing about:

- **Programming Language Design**: Insights from developing Aqua and working with functional and relational programming paradigms
- **Dataflow Systems**: Experiences with Apache Flink, streaming architectures, and distributed computing
- **Research Notes**: Summaries and reflections on recent papers and conferences
- **Technical Deep Dives**: Detailed explorations of interesting problems and solutions

## About Me

I'm currently completing my PhD at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, where I've been developing Aqua, a dataflow programming language that combines functional and relational programming concepts for stream processing.
